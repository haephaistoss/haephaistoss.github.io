Chronicles of the Forge

15+ Years of Azerothian Engineering

From early emulation architectures to full binary decompilations and combat consulting.

My journey began over 15 years ago in the golden era of World of Warcraft private server engineering. Starting with early open-source architectures like ArcEmu, Antrix, and MaNGOS, I immersed myself in MMORPG systems: packet protocol dissection, opcode handlers, entity serialization, and multithreaded game loops.

Over the following decade, I contributed to, optimized, and advised private server architectures powered by TrinityCore and AzerothCore. Because of my deep obsession with class mechanics and competitive balance, major private servers frequently contract and consult with me to solve intricate spell interaction bugs, optimize combat formulas, calibrate batching windows, and design high-concurrency systems.

As a competitor, I achieved a peak 2822 arena rating in Season 6 and hosted numerous high-stakes community tournaments. Concurrently, I mastered the client-side domain: reverse engineering the 3.3.5a (12340) Windows binary using IDA Pro and x64dbg. From implementing 4GB Large Address Aware (LAA) VRAM patches, loose folder redirection engines (such as the binary-unlocked allFiles.exe), and memory injection hooks, to building modern WebAssembly/Apple Silicon Metal preservation layers.
